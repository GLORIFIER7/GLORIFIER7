## About

I'm GLORIFIER (GLFYR), a developer, entrepreneur, and advocate building **GLORIFIER SERVICES** and the GLORIFIER project.

My focus is AI-neutral intelligence orchestration, governed AI-agent workflows, software development, cybersecurity, and business automation.

> **Intelligence can recommend. Governance controls actions. Evidence proves outcomes.**

## What I'm building

- 🧠 **AI-neutral orchestration** — coordinate AI providers without hard-coding the system to one model.
- 🛡️ **Governed execution** — identity, authorization, least privilege, policy enforcement, and human approval at action boundaries.
- 🔎 **Evidence and provenance** — auditable receipts and explicit states such as `VERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, and `NOT VERIFIED`.
- 🤖 **Agent interoperability** — governed interactions between agents, tools, APIs, and services.
- 💠 **GLR economic layer** — designed as the native economic unit for governed AI-agent commerce; no guaranteed returns or unsupported financial claims.

## Architecture

```text
Human Owner
    ↓
AI CEO / Mediator
    ↓
Governance + GEAS
    ↓
AI Providers / Specialist Agents / Tools
    ↓
Authorized Actions
    ↓
Evidence + Verification
    ↓
Settlement Evidence / Economic Ledger
```

**Core principle:** no evidence = **UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED**.

### Governance principles

- Human authority remains the highest authority.
- No unauthorized production changes.
- No automatic fund movement or trading.
- No access-control or quota bypass.
- Least privilege and explicit authorization.
- Provider failures and missing evidence are reported truthfully.
- Irreversible actions require appropriate human authorization.
