# GLORIFIER7

## Building the governed intelligence layer for AI-powered businesses

**GLORIFIER** is an AI-neutral intelligence orchestration and governed value-execution system.

> **AI intelligence produces actions. Governance controls those actions. Evidence proves the outcomes. GLR provides the economic settlement layer.**

### 🚀 What I'm building

- 🧠 **AI-neutral orchestration** — connect and coordinate multiple AI providers without hard-coding the system to one model.
- 🛡️ **Governed execution** — identity, authorization, policy enforcement, least privilege, and human approval at the action boundary.
- 🔎 **Evidence & provenance** — receipts, verification, audit trails, and explicit states such as `VERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, and `NOT VERIFIED`.
- 🤖 **Agent interoperability** — governed A2A/MCP interactions between agents, tools, APIs, and services.
- 💰 **GLR economic layer** — the native economic unit for governed AI-agent commerce and machine-to-machine settlement.

### 🏗️ Architecture

```
Human Owner
    ↓
AI CEO / Mediator
    ↓
Governance Kernel + GEAS
    ↓
Specialist Agents / Providers / Tools
    ↓
Governed Action
    ↓
Evidence + Provenance
    ↓
Economic Settlement / GLR
```

**Core principle:** no evidence = **UNKNOWN / NOT VERIFIED**.

### 🔐 Governance principles

- Human authority remains the highest authority.
- No unauthorized production changes.
- No automatic fund movement or trading.
- No access-control or quota bypass.
- Least privilege and explicit authorization.
- Provider failures are reported truthfully.
- Irreversible actions require appropriate human authority.
- Evidence is separated from intelligence and execution.

### 🪙 GLORIFIER (GLR)

**The native economic unit for governed AI-agent commerce.**

GLR is designed as an economic settlement layer for AI agents interacting with other agents, smart contracts, and Web2 APIs.

The project treats deployment and verification as evidence-backed processes rather than claims of success.
